Dots & Hexes

Draw two. Close a hex. Go again.

  • Take turns tapping two dots two steps apart. Both lines between them get drawn.
  • After the opening move, the path must touch an existing line, at either end or in the middle.
  • Close a hex to claim it, then move again.
  • When no two-line move is left, draw one line at a time. Then you move again only if your line closes two hexes at once.
  • When every line is drawn, the most hexes win.
